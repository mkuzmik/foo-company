The Gear Rebuild Program

This is the program our lifetime warranty is built on: anything we've made, at any age, gets assessed and repaired rather than replaced whenever that's possible.

  • Request a quote — email hello@ravenandridge.example with your order details and a couple of photos of the damage.
  • We assess it, free — manufacturing defects are repaired at no charge; everyday wear and tear is quoted at cost plus return shipping.
  • You ship it in — Raven's Nest members get a prepaid return label automatically.
  • We rebuild and return it — typical turnaround is 5–10 business days from the day it arrives at the workshop.

See the Warranty section of our Policies for the full terms.

Offering FAQs

What's the difference between the Rebuild Program and Second Flight?

The Rebuild Program repairs gear you still own. Second Flight is for gear you're ready to part with — you trade it in for store credit and someone else gets it refurbished.

Can I combine Second Flight credit with a Raven's Nest discount?

Yes — Raven's Nest members get an extra 10% credit bonus on top of the standard trade-in value.

Do I need my own gear for a Guided Ridge Expedition?

No. Rental gear is available for any trip if you'd rather not travel with your own pack, tent, or poles.

Is there a minimum order for wholesale partners?

Minimums are discussed case-by-case once an application is approved, based on the shop's size and region.
