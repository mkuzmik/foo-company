Beyond the Gear

Raven & Ridge is a membership as much as it's a gear shop — here's what comes with the flock.

Guided Ridge Expeditions

Small-group, multi-day guided trips led by our own field team, three seasons a year, capped at eight hikers per trip. Starting at $640.

The Gear Rebuild Program

Send back anything you bought from us, any year, and we'll repair or rebuild it at cost. Torn seam, blown zipper, worn sole — we fix it.

Second Flight Resale

Trade in gently used Raven & Ridge gear for store credit. We refurbish it and resell it at half price to keep it on trails, not in landfills.

Raven's Nest Membership

$60/year gets you free repairs, early access to limited colorways, and a seat at our annual Flock Gathering meetup.

Wholesale & Retail Partners

We supply a small number of independent outdoor shops each season. Inquiries reviewed twice a year, in spring and fall.

Ambassador Program

A yearly cohort of 12 guides, thru-hikers, and photographers who field-test unreleased gear and get a revenue share on gear they help design.

Wholesale inquiries

We take on a small number of new retail partners each season. Reach out with your shop's story and we'll follow up within two weeks.
