3. Returns & Exchanges

In short: unworn gear may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a refund; used or worn gear falls under the Rebuild Program and Warranty (Sections 4), not this section.
3.1

Except as provided in Section 3.2, a Customer may return an eligible Product within thirty (30) calendar days of the date of delivery for a refund to the original method of payment.

3.2

To be eligible for return, a Product must be unused beyond reasonable trial or fitting, in substantially the same condition as delivered, and, where reasonably practicable, accompanied by its original tags and packaging. Products marked at the time of purchase as final sale are excluded from this Section 3 and are returnable, if at all, solely for store credit at the Company's discretion.

3.3

To initiate a return, the Customer shall contact the Company at hello@ravenandridge.example with the applicable order number, following which the Company will issue return instructions and, where applicable, a return shipping label.

3.4

Refunds are issued within five (5) to ten (10) business days of the Company's receipt and inspection of the returned Product. The Company reserves the right to deny a refund, or to issue a partial refund, where a returned Product does not meet the condition requirements of Section 3.2.

3.5

A Customer wishing to exchange a Product for a different size or colorway should follow the return procedure in Section 3.3 and place a new Order for the desired Product; the Company does not process exchanges as a direct substitution.

3.6

Customers enrolled in Raven's Nest Membership are not subject to any additional restocking consideration on returns processed under this Section 3, and remain separately entitled to the repair benefits described in Section 4.4.

3.7

This Section 3 governs the return of unused merchandise only. A Product that has been used and is subsequently worn, damaged, or defective is addressed exclusively under Section 4 (Product Warranty) and the Rebuild Program, and is not eligible for a refund under this Section 3.

In short: manufacturing defects are repaired or replaced free for the life of the Product; ordinary wear and tear is repaired through the Rebuild Program, at cost, or free for Members.

The Company warrants to the original Customer that each Product shall be free from defects in materials and workmanship under normal use, for the useful life of the Product. This warranty is non-transferable and applies only to Products purchased directly from the Company or from an authorized retailer.

Where a Product is determined by the Company, in its reasonable judgment, to be defective within the meaning of Section 4.1, the Company shall, at its sole discretion, repair the Product, replace it with the same or a comparable Product, or issue store credit, at no cost to the Customer.

4.3

This warranty does not cover, and the Company shall bear no liability for: (a) damage arising from misuse, abuse, or use inconsistent with the Product's intended purpose; (b) unauthorized modification or repair by a party other than the Company; (c) ordinary cosmetic wear, including fading, minor scuffing, and pilling; or (d) damage arising from an accident, or from an act of nature.

4.4

Damage falling outside the warranty described in Section 4.1, including ordinary wear and tear such as a worn sole, a failed zipper, or a seam opened through extended trail use, is addressed under the Rebuild Program. Repairs performed under the Rebuild Program are billed to the Customer at the Company's cost of parts and labor, plus return shipping, except that such repairs are provided at no charge to Customers enrolled in Raven's Nest Membership at the time the repair is requested.

4.5

To submit a claim under this Section 4, the Customer shall contact the Company at hello@ravenandridge.example, providing the applicable order number and photographic documentation of the claimed defect or damage. Except as expressly set forth in this Section 4, the Company disclaims all other warranties, whether express or implied, to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law. In no event shall the Company's liability arising from this warranty exceed the purchase price of the applicable Product.
