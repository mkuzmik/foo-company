What Ravens Actually Remember

A look at the research behind raven cognition, and why it still shapes how we name and design gear.

Why ravens?

In 2014, founders Maren Ostby and Teo Alcazar got stranded above the tree line in a three-day storm. The only company they had for two of those days was a pair of ravens that kept circling their camp, then flying off toward the valley and back — scouting a way down that the map didn't show.

They followed the birds off the mountain. A year later, Raven & Ridge shipped its first pack.

Ravens are one of the few wild animals known to plan ahead, remember faces for years, and solve multi-step problems to get what they want. We think that's exactly the kind of company we want to keep on a trail — so it's the company we named ourselves after.

Twelve years later we're still a small team, still based near the same ridge, and every prototype is still field-tested by people who actually carry it.

Common questions

Where is Raven & Ridge based?

Our workshop and warehouse sit outside Corvid Hollow, Colorado, at the base of the same ridge where the company started.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes — we currently ship to over 30 countries. Customs and duties are calculated at checkout.

What's covered under the Rebuild Program?

Anything we've made, at any age: broken zippers, torn seams, worn-out soles, and delaminated fabric. You only pay return shipping.

How do I join Raven's Nest?

Sign up from your account page for $60/year. Membership renews annually and can be cancelled anytime.

How does Second Flight resale work?

Mail in gently used gear, we inspect and refurbish it, and you get store credit based on condition. Refurbished items are then resold at roughly half original price.

How do I apply to the ambassador program?

Applications open every October for the following year's cohort of 12. Look for the announcement in the Journal and on our socials.
