Common questions

Where is Raven & Ridge based?

Our workshop and warehouse sit outside Corvid Hollow, Colorado, at the base of the same ridge where the company started.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes — we currently ship to over 30 countries. Customs and duties are calculated at checkout.

What's covered under the Rebuild Program?

Anything we've made, at any age: broken zippers, torn seams, worn-out soles, and delaminated fabric. You only pay return shipping.

How do I join Raven's Nest?

Sign up from your account page for $60/year. Membership renews annually and can be cancelled anytime.

How does Second Flight resale work?

Mail in gently used gear, we inspect and refurbish it, and you get store credit based on condition. Refurbished items are then resold at roughly half original price.

How do I apply to the ambassador program?

Applications open every October for the following year's cohort of 12. Look for the announcement in the Journal and on our socials.

Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?

Yes, within 2 hours of placing it — after that it's usually already being packed. Email hello@ravenandridge.example with your order number as soon as possible and we'll do what we can.

Do you offer gift cards?

Yes, digital gift cards in $25, $50, $100, and $250 denominations, delivered by email and redeemable at checkout. They don't expire.

What's your return window?

30 days from delivery, unworn beyond trying it on, with original tags and packaging where possible. Full details are on our Policies page.

Do you price-match or run sales?

We run two sitewide sales a year, in spring and fall, with Raven's Nest members getting early access. We don't price-match other retailers.

What's the difference between the warranty and the Rebuild Program?

The warranty covers manufacturing defects for free, for the life of the product. The Rebuild Program covers everyday wear and tear — it's billed at cost plus return shipping, or free if you're a Raven's Nest member.

Do you offer student, military, or first-responder discounts?

Not currently. Raven's Nest membership is open to everyone at the same $60/year rate, which is where most of our ongoing savings live.

How do I find the right size?

Check the sizing guide on each pack's product page for capacity, torso fit, and weight. Still unsure? Email hello@ravenandridge.example with your height and torso length and we'll recommend a size.

My order arrived damaged — what do I do?

Email hello@ravenandridge.example within 7 days of delivery with a couple of photos and your order number. We'll send a replacement or refund, no need to return the damaged item first.

How can I track my order?

You'll get a tracking link by email as soon as your order ships. You can also see order history and tracking from your account page.

I can't log into my account — what now?

Use the "forgot password" link on the sign-in page to get a reset email. If it doesn't arrive within a few minutes, check spam or email hello@ravenandridge.example and we'll sort it out manually.
