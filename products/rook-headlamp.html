Rook Headlamp

★★★★★ 4.5 out of 5 · 203 reviews

Named for the rook, a corvid known for keeping watch after dark. The Rook throws 400 lumens on a single charge and switches to a dim red beam without fumbling for a second button.

  • USB-C rechargeable, up to 40 hours of runtime on low
  • IPX6 water-resistant housing for rain and stream crossings
  • Tilts and locks at any angle without slipping mid-hike
