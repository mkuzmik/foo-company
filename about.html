Why ravens?

In 2014, founders Maren Ostby and Teo Alcazar got stranded above the tree line in a three-day storm. The only company they had for two of those days was a pair of ravens that kept circling their camp, then flying off toward the valley and back — scouting a way down that the map didn't show.

They followed the birds off the mountain. A year later, Raven & Ridge shipped its first pack.

Ravens are one of the few wild animals known to plan ahead, remember faces for years, and solve multi-step problems to get what they want. We think that's exactly the kind of company we want to keep on a trail — so it's the company we named ourselves after.

Twelve years later we're still a small team, still based near the same ridge, and every prototype is still field-tested by people who actually carry it.
